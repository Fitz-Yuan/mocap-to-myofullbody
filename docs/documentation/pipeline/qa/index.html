Quality checks and the report

Quality checks and the report#

Every trial is graded on each check below (pipeline/qa.py measures, pipeline/report.py grades). WARN and FAIL mark something to look at, not a failed trial: nothing is dropped here. A task’s dataset builder may drop clips by its own rules (Task: box lifting). The report is ik/report.md (a table) and ik/report.json.

Check

What it measures

WARN / FAIL above

Why this limit

resid_med_mm, resid_p95_mm

body-marker residual over all samples: median, 95th percentile

12 / 20 mm; 40 / 60 mm

skin markers move 1–3 cm on soft tissue; a median above 12 mm means the model or the calibration does not fit this subject

frame_rms_med_mm

the median frame’s RMS marker error, as OpenSim’s IK reports it

20 / 30 mm

OpenSim’s IK guideline: RMS under 2 cm

frame_max_med_mm

the median frame’s largest marker error

40 / 60 mm

OpenSim’s IK guideline: largest error under 2–4 cm

limb_lost_s

the longest stretch in which the IK lost a limb: two or more of its markers more than 6 cm off, or set aside as mislabelled

0.1 / 0.5 s

a lost arm moves 3–4 of 39 markers, which the residual percentiles do not show (Tracking-loss recovery)

flips

share of frames with a joint faster than 15 rad/s

0.3 % / 1 %

no human joint turns that fast; a fit jumping between two solutions does

gap_max

the worst placed body marker’s share of missing frames

20 % / 90 %

a segment held by few markers is held by its neighbours and the joint limits

lomo_med_mm

median over markers of the leave-one-marker-out prediction error (if run, Marker prediction: leave one marker out)

20 / 30 mm

how well the other markers place a hidden one

stand_knee_deg, stand_hip_deg

knee and hip flexion in quiet standing (pelvis and ankles still for 0.5 s, pelvis at its upright height)

10 / 20°

quiet standing is near 0°; an offset here points to the calibration and shifts every frame of the subject (Joint angles: what the residual cannot show)

The per-trial grades of the case study are on The case study: box lifting with OptiTrack.
