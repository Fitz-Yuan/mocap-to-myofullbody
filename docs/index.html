Mocap to MyoFullBody
Handbook Viewer GitHub

From raw optical motion capture to a -muscle body.

Mocap to MyoFullBody reads OptiTrack recordings without Motive, calibrates each subject on a static standing trial, and solves the motion on MyoFullBody, a MuJoCo model with muscles. On the same data it matches OpenSim where it should, and fits the markers twice as closely.

01 / 05

Raw OptiTrack .tak, read without Motive.

A .tak is an OLE compound file. The reader parses the marker trajectories, labels and rigid bodies directly on Linux, and agrees with Motive's own TRC exports to mm on takes.

mmLargest difference from Motive's TRC export

02 / 05

Labels cleaned, mapped to Plug-in-Gait.

Motive's labels are mapped to the Plug-in-Gait marker set. Head markers are assigned by position, mislabelled samples are dropped, pelvis markers are refilled as a rigid body, and unlabelled points are not used. Nothing is labelled by hand.

    03 / 05

    Subject calibration on a static standing trial.

    Where each marker sits on its bone is fitted per subject. Without a static trial, marker placement and joint angles cannot be separated, and quiet standing came out with a median ° of knee flexion. Anchored on the static trial, it is °.

    Knee flexion in quiet standing, median over trials

    04 / 05

    Least-squares IK, degrees of freedom.

    Each frame is solved by bounded least squares on MyoFullBody, with a robust loss, floor contact, stance-foot locking and joint speed limits. The median marker residual is about mm.

    Degrees of freedom: pelvis, legs, lumbar spine and arms

    05 / 05

    MyoFullBody, muscles.

    The motion is written as joint angles of MyoFullBody, a MuJoCo musculoskeletal model. Exported clips open in the browser viewer with bones, muscles and markers.
